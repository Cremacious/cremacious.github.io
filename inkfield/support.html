Inkfield Support

Need help with Inkfield: Logic Puzzles, found a bug, or have an idea? Email codemackcreations@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

Purchases

If you bought Remove Ads and ads are still showing, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. If that doesn't fix it, email us.

Progress

Your level progress is stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress; we can't recover it for you.
