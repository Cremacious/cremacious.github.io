The app is free

Puzzle Ops is free to download and play. The free version is supported by ads, and every level and mission works without paying anything.

Remove Ads

You can make a one-time Remove Ads purchase. It is not a subscription, it does not renew, and it will not charge you again. It permanently removes banner ads and the full-screen ads between levels. The optional watch-an-ad rescue that clears a strike stays available — it only ever plays if you choose it.

The purchase is processed by Apple, not by us. It is tied to your Apple Account, so you can restore it on a new device with the Restore Purchases option in Settings. Refunds are handled by Apple under their standard policy; we cannot issue them ourselves. To request one, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

Your campaign progress belongs to you and is stored on your device. We have no access to it. That also means we cannot recover it: if you delete the app or lose the device, it is gone.
