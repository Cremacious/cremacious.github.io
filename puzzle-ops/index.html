Puzzle Ops Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Puzzle Ops ("the app") is a puzzle game made by an independent developer. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and how it's used.

Data we collect

Puzzle Ops does not require an account, and does not collect your name, email address, or any other information that identifies you personally. The data below is collected by the app and the third-party services it uses (Google AdMob and RevenueCat), and none of it is linked to your identity or used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

  • Device identifier — used to restore a previous Remove Ads purchase on the same device, and to serve ads through Google AdMob.
  • Purchase history — used to validate your Remove Ads purchase and keep ads switched off.
  • Advertising data — information about ads shown in the app, collected by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads.
  • Product interaction — basic signals such as app launches and whether an ad was viewed, collected by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads.
  • Crash and performance data — anonymous diagnostics such as crash logs and app launch time, collected by Google AdMob so it can tell whether its own ad code is misbehaving.

Your progress

Your campaign progress, settings and everything else you do in Puzzle Ops is stored locally on your device only. It is never uploaded to us or to any third party, and we have no access to it.

Advertising

Puzzle Ops shows non-personalized ads through Google AdMob unless you purchase Remove Ads. Because ads are non-personalized, Puzzle Ops does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not access your device's advertising identifier for tracking purposes. In the EEA and UK, Puzzle Ops asks for your consent before showing any ads, per Google's user consent policy.

Learn more about how Google's advertising products handle data: Google's Partner Sites policy.

Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed by Apple's App Store and managed with RevenueCat, a purchase infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives your anonymous purchase receipt to validate the purchase and keep ads switched off across reinstalls on the same device.

Learn more: RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Puzzle Ops is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your choices

You can purchase Remove Ads to switch off banner and full-screen ads entirely. You can also reset or limit ad tracking through your device's system settings at any time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
